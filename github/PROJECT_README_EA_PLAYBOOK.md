## 4. Features & Deliverables

### A. Executive Inbox Operating System (IOS)
* **Attention Defense Rules**: Safeguards 3.5 hours of sacred morning deep work (8:30 AM – 12:00 PM) by filtering non-critical correspondence.
* **VIP Stakeholder Directory**: Pre-approved routing rules for Board members, Sovereign Wealth Fund LPs, and General Counsel.
* **4-Tier Prioritization Matrix**: Categorizes messages into Critical, High, Medium, and Low with definitive actions (Review, Delegate, Track, Archive).

### B. Executive Morning Brief
* **Format**: Under 3 minutes reading time.
* **Components**: BLUF overview, Top 3 Immediate Executive Priorities, Delegated Work summary with assignees, Risks/Watch Items, and recommended focus blocks.

### C. The DRRN Decision Framework
A structured methodology for escalating executive decisions without dumping open-ended problems:
* **D (Decision / Recommendation)**: Concrete, decisive action proposed.
* **R (Reason)**: Direct evidentiary basis and expected operational benefit.
* **R (Risk / Trade-off)**: Realistic downside, operational costs, or secondary friction.
* **N (Next Step / Mitigation)**: Direct owner, deadline, and proactive fallback plan.

## 6. Setup & Operational Usage

This is an **operational architecture and template system**, not an executable software package. No software installation or compilation is required.

### How to Apply This Playbook:
1. **Intake & Profile Isolation**:
   * Open `templates/case_intake_template.md`. Record executive constraints, sacred calendar blocks, and communication rules. Keep executive profiles strictly separated.
2. **Execute Daily Inbox Triage**:
   * Run the incoming email batch through `templates/inbox_prioritization_matrix.md`.
   * Assign each thread an action: `Review`, `Delegate`, `Track`, or `Archive`.
3. **Draft the Morning Brief**:
   * Synthesize the top items into `templates/executive_brief_template.md`. Deliver to the executive's preferred asynchronous channel (Slack or Email) before their morning start.
4. **Escalate with DRRN**:
   * For any ambiguity requiring executive sign-off, populate `templates/drrn_decision_template.md` so the executive only needs to reply "Approved" or make one targeted choice.

## 7. Inputs, Outputs & Worked Example

### Sample Input (Scenario Excerpt)
> *"Email from Dominic Lau (Legal Counsel): Revised SPA received for Project Titan. Investment Committee meeting begins at 10:00 AM; signing deadline is 12:00 PM today."*

### Sample Output (DRRN Resolution)
* **Decision**: Stage DocuSign contract package for immediate post-IC execution at 11:15 AM; do not interrupt 8:30 AM – 10:00 AM deep prep block.
* **Reason**: Dominic confirmed all material indemnities match term sheet; executive review required only on final schedule.
* **Risk**: 45-minute window before noon deadline if IC runs late.
* **Next Step**: Stage DocuSign package; text IC coordinator to confirm hard stop at 11:00 AM. Fallback: Dominic authorized to execute escrow escrow holding letter if needed.

* **Simulated Scope**: This framework was developed and validated in simulated executive support scenarios during Athena / Oraz PH candidate preparation.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Requirement**: The framework provides decision templates; it does not replace human executive judgment or firm-specific compliance rules.
* **Confidentiality Notice**: All names, companies, and fund data in demonstrated cases are fictionalized or sanitized for training and candidate presentation.
